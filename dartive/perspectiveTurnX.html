The up/down sibling of perspectiveTurnY: rotateX turns around the horizontal axis — like a trunk lid hinged front-to-back — so the visible motion is a tilt/nod (up/down), not left/right.

The two compose by calling canvas.transform with each matrix in turn — Canvas.transform is cumulative, so no matrix multiplication is needed by the caller.

First extracted from products/squishy-mascot-twin's mascot_painter.dart, where it drove a momentary "peek" tilt independent of perspectiveTurnY's face-cycle sweep.

RearmableTimer

RearmableTimer class Motion and input

A Timer that can be re-armed (cancel the pending firing and schedule a new one) without the caller hand-tracking a nullable Timer? field and repeating the _timer?.cancel(); _timer = Timer(...) dance. Pure dart:async — no Riverpod, no scheduling policy of its own; the caller still decides durations and callbacks, arm/cancel are just the lifecycle plumbing.

First extracted from products/squishy-mascot-twin's core/state/mascot_fsm.dart, which had this exact cancel-then-create pattern hand-written three separate times (a settle timer, a sleep timeout, a blink scheduler) before extraction.
